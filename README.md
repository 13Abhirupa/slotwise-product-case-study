# SlotWise – Product Management & Technical Delivery Case Study

## Project Overview

SlotWise is a proposed SaaS platform designed to simplify appointment scheduling and daily operations for independent salons and small beauty-service businesses.

This portfolio project demonstrates an end-to-end approach to product discovery, product strategy, requirements engineering, UX design, technical architecture, development planning, quality assurance, release planning and product analytics.

## Business Problem

Small salons often manage appointments, walk-ins, staff availability and daily operations across disconnected tools or manual processes.

SlotWise explores how these workflows could be brought together in one centralised workspace.

## Product Vision

To provide salon owners and their teams with a simple, reliable workspace to manage appointments, staff schedules, walk-ins and daily business operations.

## Target Market

Independent salons and small beauty-service businesses in Delhi NCR, India.

## My Role

**Product Management | Business Analysis | Technical Delivery Planning**

This case study covers:

- Product discovery and preliminary market research
- Opportunity assessment and product strategy
- MVP definition and product roadmap
- Product requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria
- User journeys and low-fidelity wireframes
- Proposed technical architecture and system design
- Development planning and delivery governance
- QA scenarios and UAT planning
- Release, deployment and operational planning
- Product analytics and KPI framework

## Project Status

This is a self-initiated portfolio case study. Product concepts, research implications, architecture and delivery plans are documented as proposals. No production application, customer validation or live deployment is claimed.
